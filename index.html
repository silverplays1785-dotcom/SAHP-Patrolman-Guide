Internal Rules

Failure may result in Strike / Demotion / Dismissal / Detention / Deportation
  1. Rule 1: Sleeping in uniform is allowed where normal citizens can't see you sleeping (inside double locked doors).
  2. Rule 2: Everyone has to follow the dress code strictly.
  3. Rule 3: Parking of private vehicles is only allowed in the private garage.
  4. Rule 4: Everyone has to comply with IA for check except deputy and under deputy (only deputies will do checks on under deputies).
  5. Rule 5: Everyone has to take care that all doors are locked properly.
  6. Rule 6: Make sure all are aware that they must have at least 3 units to attend ATM Robberies/Emergency calls.
  7. Rule 7: Using org vehicles for personal use on duty and using personal vehicles on duty is strictly prohibited.
  8. Rule 8: Entering the Ghetto is strictly prohibited, being on duty and off duty both (including the blackmarket).
  9. Rule 9: It is forbidden to block the entrance by barricades or cars. You must leave a free pass that people can go through by driving cars.
  10. Rule 10: Nobody can ask for promotion before completing 7 days of experience in the current rank.
  11. Rule 11: High Commands are not allowed to ask for promotion.
  12. Rule 12: FL is strictly prohibited in any RP Situation/Green zone.
  13. Rule 13: Placing barricades on rooftops is strictly prohibited (make sure to remove barricades after placing them anywhere).
  14. Rule 14: Toxicity is strictly prohibited.
  15. Rule 15: Disrespecting other org and org members is strictly prohibited.
  16. Rule 16: Before doing Department chat, announce on the radio that you are doing the department chat.
  17. Rule 17: If anyone leaves the organisation before 7 days will surely get blacklisted for 14 days.
  18. Rule 18: You must always have Health Insurance and Gun License.
  19. Rule 19: Do not take part in illegal activity; no illegal items on yourself or in your vehicle.
  20. Rule 20: You must store the bodycam footage of the whole shift for a minimum of 48 hours.

Jurisdiction & Restricted Areas

Operational restriction from current guide

Restricted Area — Call Sign Ghetto

The RED area is designated as the Ghetto. DO NOT ENTER the Ghetto unless there is a Store Robbery, Ghetto Patrol or Special Operations.

10-51 Vehicle Traffic Stop Procedure

Radio call format from current guide
Traffic stop, no additional assistance required
[Badge Number] to dispatch, show me conducting a 10-51 on a [Car Color/Type], we're Code 4 on [Street]
Traffic stop, additional unit required
[Badge Number] to dispatch, show me conducting a 10-51 on a [Car Color/Type], could I get an additional unit on [Street]
Conclude the stop
[Badge Number] to dispatch, show my last 10-51 is now 10-99, show me back 10-8 on [Street]

Arrest / 10-15 Procedure

Transport and conclusion radio format
After arrest, transporting to DOC
[Badge Number] to dispatch, show me 10-17 to DOC with one 10-15 and [Number of Additionals And/Or Convoys]
Concluding the arrest
[Badge Number] to dispatch, show my last 10-15 is now 10-99, show me back 10-(8/17/19)

Miranda Rights

Read to suspect as provided
You have the right to remain silent,
Anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law.
You have the right to an attorney,
If you cannot afford an attorney, one will be appointed to you by the state if available.
Do you understand the rights I just read to you?
If the suspect doesn't respond, read the Miranda rights 3 times. If there is still no response after 3 times, the guide instructs: “Sir, I'm taking your silence as yes.”

Article 5 — Traffic Statute Crimes

5.2 Parking Violations: Refer to Code of Traffic and Vehicle Law article 6.2 for the parking violation and article 6.3 for fine amount.

5.3 Habitual Traffic Offender: A habitual traffic offender is a person who regularly and consistently violates Traffic Statutes by speeding or parking. The guide lists: three parking violations of the same type within seven days; three moving violations of the same type within seven days; or seven of any speeding/parking violations within seven days. Punishment is revocation of Driver's License, approved by law-enforcement high command or a Department of Justice judge.

Article 10 — Violations of Public Order

Misdemeanour rules

A misdemeanour does not ordinarily carry imprisonment unless multiple misdemeanours are committed. Once identified and fined for a misdemeanour, the suspect is to be released immediately. The guide says a suspect may not be searched when they have only committed a misdemeanour.

If a combination of misdemeanours exceeds 5 points, the suspect may be considered for a prison term up to 30 months, plus a maximum fine of $25,000.

Aircraft Inspection — 5.4

Sandy Shores Municipal Airfield

Pilot licence checks, drug interdiction and safety inspections of aircraft coming into Sandy Shores Municipal Airfield are to be conducted by officers certified by an SAHP-developed training program. Joint operations may be requested by the current SAHP leader.

Checks may be mandatory or random, including every third plane. The plane, pilot and anyone aboard are to be identified, checked for wanted levels/active warrants and searched for contraband.
